# Section 3 SK — Saskatchewan RM Crop Yields: ranking of E21–E25 and N21–N25

1. E22 — Best single question in the block: | and %in% as two routes to the same rows, arrange/desc, and the Crop == "Oats" & Crop == "Barley" trap as a verbal prediction; every part does distinct work and every answer is a concrete gradable value.
2. E23 — Sequential filtering with >= and &, select, arrange, and a ! rewrite; covers the most comparison machinery of any question, paced well, and (d) is a purely verbal counterfactual that grades cleanly from the script.
3. E25 — The block's mutate question: two conversions in one call, arrange on a new column, and a predict-without-running ratio check that tests understanding of what mutate did rather than whether it ran.
4. E21 — Clean foundational filter+select with the one conceptual point beginners actually need here (filter/select return new data frames, the original is untouched); modest coverage but nothing wasted.
5. N23 — The most distinct newcomer: %in% on a numeric Year column (E22 only uses it on Crop), ascending sort as a deliberate contrast to desc, and the 2021-drought reading anchors the sort output to a real interpretation.
6. N21 — Adds the one skill the E block skips, a two-sided range condition with & on Year, and the |-instead-of-& prediction is a good inverse of E22(d); loses ground because it overlaps N23 (canola, year filtering, arrange) and hands the recipe in the stem's parenthetical.
7. N22 — The revenue mutate is engaging and (d)'s rank-equivalence question is the sharpest comment part among the newcomers, but structurally it shadows E25 (filter, scalar-multiple mutate, arrange, mean) and would draw as a near-duplicate beside it.
8. E24 — The block's select-variants and rename coverage, but part (a) triples the same select and "confirm the two have the same column names" is exactly the confirm-the-count busywork the question rules say to cut.
9. N24 — Near-duplicate of E24 with heavier scaffolding (the full old-name/new-name mapping is dictated in the stem, leaving little to construct); its one real asset is the reversed-name rule check in (c).
10. N25 — Filter twice and arrange, nothing else: the narrowest verb coverage of the ten and structurally a re-skin of E23; the share-of-observations interpretation in (d) is nice but not enough to carry it.

RANKING: E22,E23,E25,E21,N23,N21,N22,E24,N24,N25
