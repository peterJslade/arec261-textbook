# Section 2 — Saskatchewan RM Canola: ranking of E11–E15 vs N11–N15

Ranked best to worst on rules fit, coverage of Section 2 skills (project folders, relative paths, read_csv, glimpse/summary, $-statistics, inspection habits), distinctness within the block, gradability from a submitted script in 8–10 minutes, and pedagogical value.

1. E11 — The block's anchor: folder sketch, relative-path read_csv, nrow/ncol/names, and the portability rationale; hits the most Section 2 skills per minute and every part is cleanly gradable.
2. E13 — summary() plus the module's inspection questions and the is-a-low-value-an-error judgment; the best pure inspection-habits question, with a genuinely good part (c).
3. E12 — glimpse, the read_csv column-spec note, and 2024 vs "2024"; distinct skill (types and the read message) that nothing else in the block covers.
4. N11 — head(), what one row represents, range() on Year, and the rows-per-year vs ~295 RMs check; a fresh unit-of-observation angle absent from the existing five, though part (d) leads the witness a little.
5. E14 — mean/median/sd/var, 90th percentile, and the skew reading; solid $-statistics work, docked only for near-identity with the sample-test Section 2 question shown in the preamble.
6. E15 — the could-not-find-read_csv error, install vs load, the quotes rule; important and distinct, but almost all comment-parts with little computation to grade, and part (d)'s name-the-four-packages is recall bordering on busywork.
7. N12 — mean/sd/10th–90th percentiles with grower-facing interpretation; well written and the 90–10 gap vs sd comment is good, but it substantially re-covers E14's ground within the same block.
8. N14 — relative-path read plus the wrong-path what-if; the missing-`data/` error prediction is its one genuine addition, but the portability rationale duplicates E11 part (d) nearly beat for beat.
9. N13 — summary() then recompute mean/median and "check they match": the confirmation part is exactly the redundant busywork the rules cut, and the rest duplicates E13 and E14; only the mean(Yield)-without-prefix part earns its place.
